## Notes & limitations

- **Video lectures** are embedded media (third-party player). Spoken-word transcripts are NOT on the pages — they are provided as downloadable decks ("Video Transcript and Summary Deck" pages). Each video page carries only a short text summary, captured here.
- **Downloadable files** (summary decks, transcripts, case PDFs, reading resources) are referenced by their Canvas file IDs (e.g. `files/6241492`). They were not downloaded — downloads require your explicit approval per file.
- The course runs 20 weeks; as of extraction, modules through Week 7 were released. Weeks 8–20 (including the Capstone) were not yet open.
- Third-party case-study text (e.g. the Polaroid article) and copyrighted readings are summarised rather than reproduced verbatim.
