# Module 10: Leveraging AI for Competitive Advantage

> Extracted from ISB / Emeritus course 17878 for personal study. Faculty: Professor Ashish. Added June 2026. (Assignment 10.1 case study is archived separately under `Assignments/10_1/`.)

## Overview & Learning Outcomes

Professor Ashish covers data- and AI-led transformation: the capabilities required for digital transformation, organisational readiness for AI, analytics maturity, explainable AI and algorithm trade-offs, and AI applications/future potential in business transformation (focusing on AgFinTech). By the end you can: evaluate a company's preparedness for AI adoption; outline a framework for monetising data and AI; explain the assets and capabilities of an AI-led company; and apply data/AI-led disruptive innovation to a fintech context.

**Key activities (due Wednesday, June 17, 2026):** Videos 1–12; Required Assignment 10.1 (Satellite Data and Fintech Innovation for Financial Inclusion). Self-study: Bonus caselet — Yongqianbao.

## Video Lectures

1. **(02:15) Module Overview** — implementation challenges companies face in digital transformation.
2. **(04:11) The Value of Data** — data as a source of revenue; why data is the key asset; how companies monetise data; the falling marginal cost of data.
3. **(13:27) Artificial Intelligence** — the role of AI and analytics in deriving insights and building novel business models; how AI evolved and how its scope may narrow in future.
4. **(monetisation)** — fueling growth through data monetisation (McKinsey reference).
5. **(05:03) Introduction to Data and AI-Led Transformation** — defining digital transformation; benefits, challenges, and essential capabilities.
6. **(05:56) Capabilities Required for Digital Transformation** — leveraging IoT and blockchain to build intellectual and market-based assets; capabilities including data curation, governance, and managerial skills that drive sustainable value.
7. **(08:58) Readiness for AI and Data-Led Transformation** — a survey assessing organisations on technology adoption and AI readiness, categorised into quadrants of the transformation journey.
8. **(02:47) Analytics Maturity** — the Analytics Maturity Model (basic → advanced analytics), turning data into actionable insight.
9. **(04:40) Explainable AI and Algorithm Trade-offs** — the trade-off between explainability and accuracy, and choosing the right model for the need.
10. **(06:22) Applications and Future of AI in Business Transformation** — AI's power is maximised when deployed across *tasks* rather than entire *jobs*; emphasises patience, comprehensive planning, and iterative experimentation.
11. **(10:04) AgFinTech** — agriculture meets fintech: AI, IoT, and blockchain transforming farming, productivity, and farmer finance through data-driven platforms.
12. **(01:12) Module Summary** — recap toward the bigger picture of disruptive innovation using data and AI.
