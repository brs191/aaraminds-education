> Extracted from ISB / Emeritus course 17878 for personal study. Faculty: Professor Himanshu. Added June 2026. (Assignment 11.2 case study is archived separately under `Assignments/11_2/`.)

## Overview & Learning Outcomes

Professor Himanshu covers implementing AI-driven digital transformation: the implementation framework (strategy), a case study on Apollo Hospitals, the organisational framework for AI adoption, developing/managing an AI-skilled workforce, and leadership/AI advocacy. By the end you can: understand the strategic, tactical, and human aspects of integrating AI; and implement AI in a way that supports the workforce and aligns with organisational values and ethics.

**Key activities (due Wednesday, June 24, 2026):** Videos 1–6; Required Assignment 11.2 (Visionary Bank's AI-Driven Fraud Detection System). Self-study: Discussion 11.1 (Building an AI-Skilled Workforce).

## Pre-read: Apollo Hospitals — The Journey of Digital Transformation

## Self-Study Discussion 11.1: Building an AI-Skilled Workforce

Organisations build AI/data literacy via four approaches — **Build** (training programs), **Buy** (hiring specialised talent), **Borrow** (contractors/gig workers), and **Bot** (automating with AI tools). *Discussion:* what strategies does your organisation use to build an AI-skilled workforce, and what challenges have you faced?
