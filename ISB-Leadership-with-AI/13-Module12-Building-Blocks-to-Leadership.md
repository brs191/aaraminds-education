# Module 12: Building Blocks to Leadership I

> Extracted from ISB / Emeritus course 17878 for personal study. Faculty: Professor Nandu. Added June 2026.

## Overview & Learning Outcomes

Professor Nandu covers how leaders take ownership of developing and executing strategy dynamically while shaping organisational culture, plus leadership communication (storytelling) and building yourself as a leader amid disruptive change. By the end you can: discover how a dynamic leader develops and executes strategies; shape an organisation's culture; understand "culture eats strategy for breakfast"; and use AI tools to track/ensure execution and for monitoring and performance evaluation.

**Key activities (due Wednesday, July 1, 2026):** Videos 1–12. Self-study: Discussion 12.1 (Navigating AI Integration in Leadership and Strategy); Bonus Content "Leading Myself" and "Leading Teams."

1. **(06:25) Apollo 13 Case Study: Overview** — crisis management through strategy, execution, and culture change.

3. **(06:18) Perception to Decision** — the third part of the execution loop ("how to make it happen"); making promises, what can go wrong, checkpoints.

7. **(11:28) Reimagining Customer Value and Capabilities with AI** — using AI to enhance customer value and refine strategy; three frameworks for navigating disruption including the **Gartner AI Opportunity Radar** and dynamic execution loops.
8. **(06:31) Are We Born to Be the First Movers?** — leadership's impact on teams; **cognitive diversity** and **psychological safety**.
9. **(04:28) How to Create Culture?** — creating culture in an organisation.
10. **(06:34) Nurturing Innovation: Behavioural Enablers** — breaking the innovation barrier; the **DBS Bank "BEANs"** case for behavioural/cultural transformation.
11. **(13:19) Measured Transformation: Nestlé Philippines Case Study** — Prof. Nandu's CEO experience: the 2005 crisis, its management, and transformational changes (team dynamics, layoffs, leadership changes).
12. **(08:19) Culture as Continuous Change and Preparation for Change** — how culture affects strategy implementation.

## Bonus Content: Leading Myself (8 videos)

Dynamics of disruption; responding to disruption (threat vs. opportunity, not danger); leadership strategy and the leader's role in creating disruption; the leader's posture / change mindset; **leadership vs. management**; the impact of AI on leadership (incl. the Nadella–Schwab Davos 2024 conversation); key leadership behaviours for sustainability; and the characteristics of successful leaders.

## Bonus Content: Leading Teams (8 videos)

Mastering networks (types and their role in change); preparing for change (the comfort zone and resistance); communicate for action (the importance of "Why"); communicate for impact (**storytelling** and the elements of a successful story); leveraging AI to enhance communication; celebrate ethics in storytelling; the art of storytelling in business; and "leadership is a habit."

## Self-Study Discussion 12.1: Navigating AI Integration in Leadership and Strategy

Frameworks such as the **Gartner AI Opportunity Radar** and **Don Sull's dynamic strategy-execution loop** help integrate AI effectively while maintaining customer value and adaptability. *Discussion:* the most significant challenge leaders face integrating AI, and how you'd address it using one of the frameworks.
