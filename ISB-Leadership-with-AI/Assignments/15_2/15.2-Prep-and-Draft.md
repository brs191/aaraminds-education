## Module 15 grounding (use this framing)

AI-Enabled Transformational Leadership has four levels: **Level 3 Organisational**, **Level 2 Change Management**, **Level 1 Personal**, **Level 0 Foundational**. Level 3 uses the **PICPOP** organisational framework and rests on three human–AI collaboration modes:

1. **Humans augment machines** (train, explain, sustain AI).
2. **Machines augment humans** (amplify, interact, embody).
3. **Reimagine business processes** — the biggest value, worth trillions, concentrated in **customer management** and **supply chain management**.

The **operating model** has three components — **data pipeline, algorithms, software infrastructure** — plus an **experimentation platform** before deployment (Microsoft example). The **REAL approach** describes the stages, activities, and barriers to becoming an AI-first company.

## Draft: AT&T AI-Enabled Business Model (function by function)

**Framing statement.** AT&T Business Technology Product Development runs the Sales Tooling ecosystem (Salesforce, ROME, ADOPT, SSP, Business Center) that powers business-critical sell-market-order-manage flows. The AI-enabled model reimagines these functions through human–AI collaboration — not automation alone — to deliver more engineering and business outcomes at lower cost (the ~15% budget-reduction mandate), faster go-to-market, and higher quality.

| Business function | AI-enabled reimagining (AT&T) | Collaboration mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer management / Sales & Ordering** | GenAI assistants over Salesforce/ROME/ADOPT/SSP to guide sellers on pricing, contracting, and configuration; recommend the right plan/product; conversational order support | Machines augment humans |
| **Supply chain / Provisioning & fulfilment** | AI for order-fallout prediction, provisioning-delay detection, and interface/dependency mapping across ADI, AVPN, B-VOIP to cut turnaround | Reimagine process |
| **Product development / Engineering (SDLC)** | Business Analyst, Scrum Master, and QA agents; a Code Intelligence Factory that reverse-engineers apps into reusable BRDs/HLDs/LLDs across Pricing, Contracting, Credit Check, Billing | Machines augment humans + humans augment machines (review) |
| **Operations / Production support** | Azure Logs Analysis agent + incident-pattern prediction to triage before escalation; Grafana dashboard / UI-analytics builders | Machines augment humans |
| **Marketing & customer engagement** | GenAI for campaign copy, personalisation, and multilingual customer communication | Machines augment humans |
| **Workforce / HR** | Skill-mapping and Project/Program Planner agents integrating Jira, SharePoint, and employee-skill data for resource alignment | Reimagine process |
| **Finance / FinOps** | Azure Cost / FinOps optimiser for cloud-spend reduction; measurable against the budget mandate | Machines augment humans |

**Operating model (how it runs).** *Data pipeline* — consolidate telemetry, tickets, design docs, and Sales Tooling data with governance and access control; *Algorithms* — ML/LLM models for prediction, retrieval, and generation using AT&T-enabled tools (GitHub Copilot, Claude, GPT); *Software infrastructure* — Azure-first, multi-vendor, integrated with legacy systems; *Experimentation platform* — a governed PoC/eval harness (building on your MCP-server PoC and coding-practice benchmarks) before production, with human-in-the-loop review for customer- and network-impacting outputs.
