## Draft: Kotter 8-Step Plan to Make AT&T (Sales Tooling org) AI-Enabled

**1. Create a sense of urgency.** The development centre must deliver more outcomes on a ~15% lower budget while competitors and AT&T peers adopt AI-assisted engineering. Frame the urgency as: *without AI-enabled engineering, we cannot meet delivery, quality, and go-to-market expectations at the new cost base.* Use concrete pain — cycle time, production-support effort, documentation gaps.

**2. Build a guiding coalition.** A cross-functional coalition: an empowered executive sponsor, engineering leaders, product owners, security/governance, and the **AI SPOCs** you established. This mirrors the coalition Nadella built around the growth-mindset shift.

**3. Form a strategic vision and initiatives.** Vision: *AT&T Sales Tooling becomes an AI-augmented engineering organisation where knowledge is easy to find, reuse, and apply across business-critical platforms.* Initiatives = the AI use-case roadmap (your 13 use cases: BA/QA/Scrum agents, Code Intelligence Factory, Azure Logs Analysis, FinOps optimiser) sequenced over three quarters.

**4. Enlist a volunteer army (communicate the vision).** AI awareness sessions, tech demos, and knowledge-sharing forums so adoption is pulled, not pushed. Address the **elephant (emotion)** — fear of job loss — with an explicit *augmentation-not-replacement* message, and the **rider (reason)** — show credible PoCs and benchmarks (your MCP-server PoC).

**5. Enable action by removing barriers.** Provide enablement (GitHub Copilot, Claude, GPT access), reference coding scaffolds/custom instructions, data access with governance, and time to experiment. Remove approval and tooling friction; create new roles (AI SPOCs, MLOps).

**6. Generate short-term wins.** Ship quick, visible wins on non-critical, high-effort work first — unit-test generation, SonarQube coverage, documentation assistance, log-analysis triage — and publicise measurable time saved. This builds belief, as early Microsoft wins did.

**7. Sustain acceleration.** Use early wins to fund and scale the harder use cases (Code Intelligence Factory, production-support agents); institute model-governance, human-in-the-loop review, and an experimentation/eval platform so scaling stays safe. Keep raising the ambition.

**8. Institute (anchor) change in the culture.** Make AI-augmented engineering the default way of working: reference practices in onboarding, career paths, and reviews; celebrate agent success stories; embed a growth mindset so the change survives staff rotation — the Nadella lesson that culture, not tools, sustains transformation.
